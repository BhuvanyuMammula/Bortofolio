Welcome to OOB MUN

OOB Model United Nations is a student-led conference dedicated to fostering diplomacy, critical thinking, and meaningful dialogue. Bringing together passionate delegates from diverse backgrounds, OOB MUN provides a platform to debate global challenges, develop leadership skills, and collaborate on innovative solutions in an engaging and intellectually stimulating environment.

01 — Committees

Eight Committees. One Conference.

UNSC, PCC, INTERPOL, COP30, CSW, GA1, ECOSOC & HCR — each bringing a distinct agenda, level of complexity, and diplomatic challenge for delegates of every experience level.

02 — Student Led

Run Entirely By Students.

Every committee, every session, every ceremony — organised from the ground up by students, for students, with zero outside direction.

03 — Experience

Diplomacy. Debate. Leadership.

OOB MUN is built to sharpen the skills that matter beyond the conference floor — negotiation, public speaking, and confident leadership under pressure.
